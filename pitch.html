Growth hypothesis

FinWise is getting enough people into trials to learn. The break happens after trial start: only 1.99% become paid customers.

If FinWise gets new trial users to import data, see a personalized cash-flow forecast, and share it with an accountant, bookkeeper, or co-owner in week one, trial-to-paid conversion should move from about 2.0% toward 2.4% to 2.6%.

The test is a 2-week A/B read with trial-to-paid conversion as the business metric, first-forecast completion as the activation signal, and day-1 drop-off as the guardrail.

The bet

Bet on a collaboration loop, not more paid acquisition. June 2024 had the most visits, 9,426, but the fewest paid conversions, 6. More traffic is not reliably creating paid accounts. A shared forecast gives the owner and their finance partner a reason to return together.

Mechanic + rationale

Weekly Forecast Readiness is a progress bar that turns the first cash-flow forecast into a weekly review habit. It fits because the behavior has a clear completion state: update data, review the forecast, test one scenario, share it with a collaborator, and confirm the next financial action. Slide 9 · The Validation

Experiment

Mini experiment brief
Standard A/B test

Method + why

Standard A/B test. It fits because we are testing one guided onboarding path against the current reverse-trial experience, with new trial users randomized at account creation and an activation readout within 24 hours.

Hypothesis + primary metric

If FinWise replaces generic trial onboarding with a guided first-forecast path, more trial users will complete a first modeling output within 24 hours. Primary metric: first modeling output completion rate within 24 hours. Success threshold: 20% relative lift over control.

Guardrail + read date

Guardrail: day-1 onboarding drop-off must not increase by more than 5% relative to control. Read date: after the two-week A/B test window closes, with no early peeking.

+5%maximum allowed day-1 drop-off increase

Growth stage + model

Stage 2 Revenue Expansion · Tiered subscription with a reverse-trial entry path. FinWise has PMF at $10M ARR, but trial-to-paid conversion is only 1.99% and one-year paid retention is 40%, so the pricing model should charge for the recurring forecast workflow after users see value.

Supporting signal

Success should show up first in first modeling output completion within 24 hours and forecast share rate. The lagging proof is trial-to-paid conversion moving from about 2.0% toward 2.4% to 2.6%.

Recommendation + bet

Give each new trial user one personalized cash-flow forecast and one collaborator share before the paywall. Gate paid plans around the ongoing workflow: Weekly Forecast Readiness, recurring data sync, saved scenarios, collaborator comments, and additional shared forecasts. This validates a packaging bet, not a price increase.

Slide 11 · The Story

Insights + Submit

Certification submission

Biggest friction

The biggest friction was resisting the obvious-looking fixes: more acquisition spend or a pricing change. I solved it by forcing every decision back through the funnel data, which showed that trial users were starting but not reaching a useful forecast fast enough to pay.

Key Aha moment

My key Aha moment was realizing that FinWise's value is not the dashboard. It is the moment a small-business owner sees a specific cash-flow forecast from their own data and has a reason to share it with a trusted financial collaborator.

Course takeaways

The main takeaway is that experimentation works best as a connected system. The funnel diagnosis shaped the growth bet, the Aha moment shaped onboarding, the engagement mechanic shaped retention, and the pricing memo showed when not to use pricing as the first lever.
