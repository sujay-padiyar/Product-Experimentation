## Recommendation

Move the paid boundary to after the first proof of value.

Every new trial user should get one personalized cash-flow forecast and one collaborator share before the paywall. Paid plans should gate the ongoing workflow:

- Weekly Forecast Readiness
- Recurring data sync
- Saved scenarios
- Collaborator comments
- Additional shared forecasts

This is a packaging bet, not a price increase. FinWise should validate where the free-to-paid boundary belongs before asking users to pay more.

_____

## Model signal

The recommendation targets two Module 4 leading indicators:

- First modeling output completion within 24 hours
- Forecast share rate with an accountant, bookkeeper, or co-owner

If the packaging change works, more trial users should reach a first forecast, share it, and then convert when the ongoing workflow becomes paid. The lagging proof is trial-to-paid conversion moving from about 2.0% toward the Module 1 target of 2.4% to 2.6%.

## Pricing memo

To: FinWise Leadership
From: Senior PM
Subject: Pricing recommendation for FinWise

FinWise is in Stage 2: Revenue Expansion. We have PMF at $10M ARR, but the current monetization path is not converting enough activated trial users into paid customers. Across 13 months, 6,424 trial starts produced only 128 paid conversions, a 1.99% trial-to-paid rate. The biggest funnel loss is trial to paid, where 98.01% of trial users leave before converting. One-year paid retention is also weak at 40%, which means pricing cannot cover up an activation and habit problem.

I recommend keeping FinWise on a tiered subscription model with a reverse-trial entry path. That model fits how FinWise delivers value: recurring cash-flow forecasting, weekly readiness checks, synced financial data, saved scenarios, and collaboration with an accountant, bookkeeper, or co-owner. A transaction model does not fit because FinWise does not monetize a single payment event. Advertising would break trust in a finance product. Pure freemium would risk giving away the repeated workflow FinWise needs to charge for.

The pricing change is packaging, not a price increase. Every new trial user should get one personalized cash-flow forecast and one collaborator share before the paywall. Paid plans should gate the ongoing workflow: Weekly Forecast Readiness, recurring data sync, saved scenarios, collaborator comments, and additional shared forecasts.

Hypothesis: if FinWise places the paywall after the first forecast and collaborator share, then more trial users will reach the Module 4 leading indicators, first modeling output completion within 24 hours and forecast share rate. If those indicators move, trial-to-paid conversion should move from about 2.0% toward the Module 1 target of 2.4% to 2.6%.

I would not raise prices yet. FinWise has not proven enough repeat value with only 40% one-year retention. The next pricing bet should validate where the free-to-paid boundary belongs, then price expansion can follow once weekly forecasting becomes a retained habit.
